# Ebb privacy policy

Last updated 30 September 2026.

Ebb is a menstrual cycle tracker that runs entirely on your device.

**What Ebb collects:** nothing. Ebb has no user accounts and no servers. It does not use analytics, crash reporting services, advertising identifiers or tracking of any kind.

**Where your data lives:** everything you log is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, protected by your device's encryption. You can add a PIN or Face ID / fingerprint lock inside the app.

**Apple Health and Health Connect:** if you choose to import, Ebb reads cycle data (periods, spotting, cervical mucus, ovulation and pregnancy tests, symptoms, basal body temperature) and, separately, sleep and resting heart rate. Access is read-only, only happens when you tap the import button, and the copy stays on your device. You can revoke access in the Health app or Health Connect at any time and remove the imported copy in Ebb.

**Imports from other apps:** files you pick (for example a Flo export) are read on the device and never uploaded.

**Optional model downloads (food photo recognition, Android):** if you choose to download an on-device model, the download request goes to the model's file host. No diary data is included in that request.

**Exports:** backups and spreadsheets are files you create and share yourself. Where you send them is under your control.

**Deleting your data:** Settings → Data → Delete all data removes everything from the device. Uninstalling the app does the same.

**Children:** Ebb is not directed at children under 13.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the new version ships with the app update and the date above is updated.

**Contact:** https://github.com/TjebbeHB/ebb/issues (please do not include personal health information in a public issue).

The published version of this policy lives at https://tjebbehb.github.io/ebb/privacy.html.
