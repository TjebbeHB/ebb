## Description

This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Ebb is a free menstrual cycle tracker that keeps everything on your phone: no account, no subscription, no ads, no analytics, no data leaves the device.

TRACK WHAT MATTERS
Periods and spotting, flow intensity, 30+ symptoms, moods, energy, pain and its impact on your day, sleep, discharge, basal body temperature, ovulation and pregnancy tests, sex and libido, pill, medication, water, exercise, weight, custom tags and notes. Hide any tracker you do not use.

PREDICTIONS THAT LEARN FROM YOU
The next period with a realistic date range, the fertile window, estimated ovulation and premenstrual days. Predictions use the median of your recent cycles, ignore outliers and cycles you exclude, and tell you how confident they are. Temperature shifts, ovulation tests and cervical mucus refine the estimate and teach Ebb your own luteal length.

MADE FOR YOUR SITUATION
Track your cycle, try to conceive, avoid pregnancy with a wider cautious window, or track bleeding on hormonal birth control with pill reminders.

INSIGHTS, NOT NOISE
Average cycle and period length, regularity, cycle history, symptoms that recur before or during your period, symptom frequency by phase, temperature curve, and gentle food and movement ideas for each phase.

BRING YOUR HISTORY
Import your Flo export file, or read periods, symptoms, tests and temperatures from Apple Health. Restore an Ebb backup on a new phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
PIN and Face ID lock. Reminder texts are deliberately vague on the lock screen. Export a JSON backup or CSV spreadsheet, or delete everything, at any time. Optional Apple Health access is read-only.

Predictions are estimates. Do not rely on them as contraception. Talk to a healthcare professional about anything that worries you.
