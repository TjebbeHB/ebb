# TestFlight test information

**Beta App Description**
Ebb is a free, private period and cycle tracker. Nothing leaves your phone: no account, no ads, no analytics. Log periods, symptoms and moods, see predictions and insights, and bring your history from Flo or Apple Health.

**What to Test** (build 5, version 1.4.0)
1. Onboarding: try "I already use Flo or Apple Health" to import your history, or go through the steps and mark your last period.
2. Today: does the cycle day, phase and next-period prediction match what you expect? Tap Log today and log a few things.
3. Calendar: tap days to see entries; use Edit period days to fix dates.
4. Insights: check averages and patterns once you have a few cycles.
5. Settings → Reminders: enable one and see if it arrives at the chosen time.
6. Settings → Privacy: set a PIN and Face ID, close and reopen the app.
7. Settings → Data: export a backup, then Delete all data and restore the backup.
8. Anything confusing, slow, mis-worded or ugly: tap the TestFlight screenshot feedback and tell us.

Known limits in this build: food photo recognition is Android-only; pregnancy and perimenopause modes are not included yet.
